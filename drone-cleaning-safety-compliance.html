Drone Cleaning Safety & Compliance

CAA-authorised drone operations, insured services and site-specific planning. Professional exterior cleaning with safety at the centre of every project.

01

CAA-authorised operations

DroneReach operates under a Civil Aviation Authority Operational Authorisation. Every flight is planned and carried out within the scope, conditions and limits of our permissions, with site suitability assessed before work begins.

Qualified remote pilots conduct our operations. Planning includes airspace checks and any additional permissions required for the location and proposed work.

02

Fully trained and qualified pilots

Our remote pilots hold the qualifications required for the operations they conduct and receive training in the aircraft, cleaning equipment and procedures used on site.

Aircraft-specific training covers routine operation and emergency procedures. The pilot and ground crew coordinate equipment, working areas and communication throughout the job.

03

Insured and accountable

Our operations are supported by aviation liability and public liability insurance appropriate to the work we undertake. Evidence of cover is available as part of your contractor approval process.

We review your organisation’s project-specific insurance requirements before agreeing the work and provide relevant evidence of cover for your assessment.

04

Site-specific risk assessments and method statements

Every project is supported by site-specific risk assessments and method statements, setting out how the work will be delivered safely and efficiently.

  • Building layout, condition and access.
  • Occupants, neighbouring properties and public areas.
  • Controlled working areas and pedestrian or vehicle management.
  • Equipment positioning and hose management.
  • Coordination with the client’s site team.
  • Emergency arrangements.
05

Aircraft checks and operational controls

Our aircraft and cleaning equipment are inspected before use and maintained in line with manufacturer requirements. We assess weather and site conditions throughout the operation and pause work whenever conditions fall outside safe operating limits.

Pre-flight checks, battery management, crew communication and agreed emergency procedures form part of our operational controls. The crew keeps site conditions under review while work is underway.

06

Protecting your building

We select cleaning methods to suit the material, condition and requirements of your building. Pressure, temperature and cleaning products are assessed carefully, with test areas used where appropriate.

Our assessment considers cladding, glass, roof surfaces, coatings, seals and sensitive architectural details. For heritage buildings, we coordinate with the client and relevant specialists, confirming any necessary consents before work. Suitability depends on the individual surface and proposed method; drone cleaning is not appropriate for every heritage building.

07

Environmental and chemical controls

We assess how cleaning activities could affect surrounding areas and put appropriate controls in place for chemical handling, spray drift and runoff.

  • COSHH assessments where applicable.
  • Suitable product selection and controlled application.
  • Drainage and runoff management.
  • Protection of vegetation and nearby sensitive areas.
  • Site-specific containment or collection where required.
08

Ready for your contractor approval process

Our team can provide the documentation your organisation needs to assess and approve the proposed work.

  • Evidence of relevant CAA operating authorisation.
  • Insurance certificates.
  • Relevant pilot qualifications.
  • Site-specific risk assessments and method statements.
  • COSHH assessments and product safety information where applicable.

Your safety questions, answered.

Are your drone operations CAA authorised?

Yes. Our operations are conducted under our CAA Operational Authorisation and within its scope, conditions and limits. We assess the site and arrange any additional permissions required before work begins.

Are your pilots trained and qualified?

Our remote pilots hold the qualifications relevant to their operations and receive training in the aircraft, cleaning equipment and operating procedures used, including emergency procedures and crew coordination.

Are you insured?

Our operations are supported by aviation liability and public liability insurance. Certificates are available on request, and we review project-specific insurance requirements before agreeing the work.

Can our building remain occupied?

This is often possible, subject to the site assessment. Entrances, windows, ventilation and working areas may need temporary controls, which we agree with your site team before work.

How do you protect pedestrians and vehicles?

We establish controlled working areas, appropriate signage and access arrangements. Coordination with your site team helps manage pedestrian routes, vehicles and nearby occupied areas.

What happens if the weather is unsuitable?

We monitor weather and site conditions throughout the operation. Work is paused or rescheduled when conditions fall outside safe operating limits.

Can you clean listed or heritage buildings?

Suitability depends on the material, condition, proposed method and any required consents. We coordinate with the client and relevant specialists where appropriate; not every heritage surface is suitable for drone cleaning.

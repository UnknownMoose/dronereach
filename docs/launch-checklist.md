# Internal launch verification

Keep this checklist in developer documentation, never in public page content.

Before launch, check every homepage trust statement and the Safety & Compliance page against the final business documents and operating procedures:

- Verify the CAA Operational Authorisation is current and covers the proposed aircraft, activity, operating scope, conditions and limits.
- Verify relevant remote pilot qualifications and equipment/aircraft-specific training, emergency procedures and crew coordination records.
- Verify aviation liability and public liability insurance cover the proposed work. Check client-specific requirements against the certificates and policy terms.
- Verify that site-specific risk assessments and method statements address access, the public, neighbouring property, controlled working areas, equipment/hoses, site-team coordination and emergency arrangements.
- Verify aircraft inspection, maintenance, battery management, weather limits and pre-flight procedures against actual manufacturer requirements and operating procedures.
- Verify material assessments, test areas, heritage consent and specialist coordination processes.
- Verify COSHH, product selection, chemical handling, spray drift, drainage/runoff, vegetation protection and containment/collection procedures where applicable.
- Confirm the team can supply all documentation described for contractor approval. Do not publish invented approval numbers, qualification names, policy limits, badges or document downloads.

The public copy uses the supplied present-tense wording. This internal checklist does not establish that the underlying permissions, qualifications, insurance or records are in place.

Also substantiate the supplied homepage speed claim, replace illustrative concept imagery with approved DroneReach assets, and hide the customer-review placeholder for launch until an approved review is available.

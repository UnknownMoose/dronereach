Your questions, answered.

Is my building suitable for drone cleaning?

Suitability depends on the building’s height, materials, condition and surroundings. We assess the site before recommending a cleaning approach.

Could cleaning damage the façade?

An unsuitable cleaning method can damage a surface. We assess the material and condition, select an appropriate approach and identify where a test area is needed before proceeding.

Will the building need to close during cleaning?

Some buildings may remain in use, although temporary exclusion zones or access restrictions may be needed. We agree these arrangements with you before work starts.

Do you need access to water on site?

We confirm water and access requirements during the assessment so you know what needs to be available before the cleaning day.

How is the price calculated?

Pricing depends on the area, surface material, condition, access and site requirements. Share the building’s location and a few photos to help us assess the work.
